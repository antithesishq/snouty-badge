# M1 hand-off notes

Tracks and their reports are summarised here for the record.

- Tracer (`trace.zig`, `camera.zig`, `scene.zig`, `water.zig`): depth is a
  comptime parameter, so the bounded recursion compiles to straight-line
  code; everything inlines into `update`. Rays leaving the sphere skip the
  sphere test, rays leaving the water skip the water test. Worst pixel
  (water -> sphere -> water -> sky) is ~350 flops, ~6 divides, ~4 sqrts,
  roughly 500-600 cycles; sky pixels ~70 cycles; typical water ~300.
  Estimated frame average 200-300 cycles/pixel against 366 at 20 fps:
  borderline, hardware decides.
- Dither (`dither.zig`): 16 instructions per pixel, one table load, no
  branch; modes `bayer_temporal` (default) and `none` (B toggles). Linear,
  no gamma yet.
- Overlay (`overlay.zig`, `-Ddebug_overlay=true`): "  1234us  20fps B" top
  left; costs 3.2 KB of `.text` (mostly the OS font).
- Float check (`zig build check-float`, `tools/check_float.mjs`): parses
  the ELF symbol table for compiler-rt soft-float names and the ARM
  attributes; verified to fail on a deliberately added f64 expression.
- Reference (`tools/reference.py`, `tools/check_render.mjs`): PLAN.md made
  executable in numpy; frames 0 and 300 pass with 1 pixel over 1 unit,
  max 3 units. A sweep over a full orbit passed; frame 500 touches the
  6-unit cap at a grazing sphere-edge pixel, so a one-pixel FAIL there is
  precision, not shading.

## Emulated cost (2026-09-27)

Measured with `tools/emu/run.sh` (unicorn Cortex-M33, cycle model in
`tools/emu/README.md`: 1 cycle for most instructions, VDIV/VSQRT 14,
loads/stores 2, LDRD 3, PUSH/POP 1+N, VFMA 3, taken branch +1; zero-wait
SRAM, no contention or stalls, so a lower bound). Frames in dither mode
`none`; all PASS against `tools/reference.py`.

| Run (m1 tag) | Insns/frame | Modelled cycles | Cyc/px | ms at 150 MHz |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| bench ELF, frame 0 | 5,365,617 | 7,295,583 | 356.2 | 48.6 |
| bench ELF, frame 300 | 5,169,143 | 7,079,328 | 345.7 | 47.2 |
| real ELF, frame 0 | 5,457,772 | 7,359,615 | 359.4 | 49.1 |

Orbit sweep (real ELF, frames 0..575 step 25): 344.5-364.8 cyc/px, worst
frame 550; the bench ELF gives 341.4-361.7 over the same frames. Against
the 50 ms budget at 20 fps (366 cyc/px) the model leaves almost no margin,
before bus contention and FP stalls, hence the M1.1 performance pass.

Per-class cost, bench frame 0, modelled cycles per pixel: sky 150,
sky + glow 184, sphere -> sky 218, water -> sky 413 (445 with sun),
water -> sphere -> sky 477, sphere -> water -> sky 486,
water -> sphere -> water -> sky 719. The two water -> sky classes are 45%
of the screen and 55% of the frame's cycles.


## Performance pass M1.1 (2026-09-27)

Every lossless change from the emulated cycle model was applied (PLAN.md
"M1.1"). Lossless means `check_render.mjs` passes against the unchanged
reference on all 600 orbit frames (max 5 units on one pixel of frame 159,
typical max 1 to 3). Modelled cost, real ELF, whole `update()`:

| | before (m1) | after (m1.1) |
|---|---|---|
| frame 0, cycles / px | 359.4 | 247.9 |
| frame 0, ms at 150 MHz | 49.1 | 33.8 |
| worst orbit frame (550), ms | 49.8 | 34.9 |
| `.text` | 13.7 KB | 97.3 KB |

The `.text` growth is comptime tables: 1/|ray| per pixel (40 KB, could be
mirrored in y to save 20 KB), primary-ray water hit distance and fade per
row (27.5 KB), paired sine table (8 KB), exact orbit sin/cos (4.8 KB).
Budget is 120 KB; M2 art (shore texture 8 KB) still fits, but the mirror
trick is the first thing to do if space gets tight.

Kept changes, in order of payoff: comptime 1/|ray| table replacing the
per-pixel sqrt+div; per-column conservative sphere screen span so most
primary rays skip the sphere test (verified with a debug paint of any
out-of-span hit over 600 frames: none); primary water hit and fade from
tables (no divide); paired sine table with the quarter turn folded into
the per-frame phases; comptime folding of constant products and affine sky
segments; one reciprocal for depth>0 water hit distance and fade;
water-origin and stable primary sphere discriminants (the stable one costs
4 cycles per pixel and is kept for precision at grazing sphere-edge
pixels); `@min(1, c)` for saturate; `noinline render_frame` plus forced
inline `trace`. Reverted as measured slower: bool hit flag, branchless sky
kink, table-picked sky segment, glow clamp removal.

Fixed on the way: the camera basis came from the interpolated sine table
and was off by about 5e-6, enough to tilt grazing rays; frame 250 failed
the reference by 8 units on one pixel. The orbit is now an exact comptime
table.

Not done, for a later A/B against appearance: 80x64 render upscaled
through the dither; far depth-2 water as plain sky; interpolating ripple
normals between rows.
